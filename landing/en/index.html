Read on your e-reader

How TabTome works

Click the extension icon → select tabs or paste your own text → TabTome saves the text, links, lists, tables, and images to an EPUB → emails the book to your e-reader.

TabTome runs on your computer — no cloud required

Your data stays on your computer

  • the browser passes the selected tab content to the TabTome app;
  • the app removes clutter and ads, processes images, and builds the EPUB;
  • TabTome settings, EPUB send records, and your encrypted email password stay in Windows on your computer. They are not sent to the developer.

Where the book goes

  • your email service receives the message with the finished EPUB;
  • the Kindle or PocketBook service delivers the book to your e-reader.

The TabTome developer receives no articles, books, email addresses, passwords, send history, or analytics. Read the full privacy policy.

Install TabTome and send browser pages to your e-reader

  1. Install the extension from Mozilla Add-ons.
  2. Download and install the TabTome app from GitHub Releases.
  3. Open the app from the Start menu and enter your email address and e-reader delivery address: name@kindle.com for Kindle or name@pbsync.com for PocketBook.
  4. For Kindle, add the sender address to Amazon's approved list. For PocketBook, confirm it from the email message if the address is not already allowlisted.
  5. Restart the browser and send your first article.
