One article or up to 25 tabs in one EPUB

Select tabs from the list or paste your own material. When you paste formatted text, TabTome keeps links, lists, tables, and images.

TabTome processes pages on your PC

TabTome does not send source pages to the developer's server. Only the data needed to retrieve images and deliver the book goes online.

Stays on your PC

  • Firefox passes the selected tab content to the TabTome app;
  • the app cleans the text, processes images, and builds the EPUB;
  • Windows stores settings, send records, logs, and your encrypted email password.

Goes online

  • your email service receives the message with the finished EPUB;
  • Amazon or PocketBook receives the book;
  • the source site may receive a request to retrieve an image.

The TabTome developer receives no articles, books, email addresses, passwords, send history, or analytics. Read the full privacy policy.

What to know before installing

TabTome works in Firefox on 64-bit Windows. Firefox blocks access to some pages, and TabTome does not carry every website element into an EPUB.

  • Works in Firefox 142+ on Windows 10 1809 or newer.
  • Sends to Kindle and PocketBook addresses ending in @pbsync.com.
  • Lets you remove images before sending.
  • Processes up to 30 images per article and 15 MB of source images per send.
  • Does not access private windows.
  • Does not extract Firefox internal pages, built-in PDFs, or add-on store pages.
  • Does not transfer video, forms, fonts, colors, or interactive website elements.
  • The Chrome and Chromium version is experimental and installed manually.

Install once and send from Firefox

The extension selects pages. The TabTome app builds the EPUB, stores email settings, and runs only while sending.

  1. Install the signed extension from Mozilla Add-ons when the public page opens.
  2. Install the TabTome app from GitHub Releases.
  3. Open the app from the Start menu and enter your email and e-reader address.
  4. Add your email address to the Amazon or PocketBook approved-sender list.
  5. Restart Firefox and send your first article.
